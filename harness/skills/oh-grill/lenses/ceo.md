# CEO Lens

Strategic perspective: is this the right thing to build?

## Questions

1. **Market need** — Who specifically needs this? What's the evidence?
2. **Narrowest wedge** — What's the smallest version that delivers value?
3. **10-star product** — What would make this a 10 instead of a 5?
4. **Status quo** — What do people do today instead? Why is that painful?
5. **Premise challenge** — Is the framing itself wrong? (See: "daily briefing app" → "chief of staff AI")
6. **Effort/value ratio** — Is this the highest-leverage thing to build right now?

## Output

- Prioritized concerns (critical / notable / minor)
- Score 0-10 with justification
- Recommended scope mode: expansion / selective / hold / reduce
