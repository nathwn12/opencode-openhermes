# oh-brainstorm

## When to Use
When exploring a new idea, validating market fit, or designing a feature from scratch. Two modes depending on context.

## Modes
### Startup Mode (new product)
Six forcing questions:
1. **Demand reality** — who specifically needs this?
2. **Status quo** — what do they do today?
3. **Desperate specificity** — what's the one concrete thing they can't do?
4. **Narrowest wedge** — what's the smallest useful version?
5. **Observation** — what will you see/hear when it works?
6. **Future-fit** — does this compound or does it plateau?

### Builder Mode (side project)
Design thinking for personal projects: learning goals, curiosity-driven exploration, what would be fun to build and show off.

## Output
A structured design doc with the idea, market analysis, and execution plan.

## Anti-patterns
- Building before validating demand
- Perfecting the pitch before understanding the problem
- Skipping narrowest wedge (overbuilding v1)
